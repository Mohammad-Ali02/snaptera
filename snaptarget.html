What it does

Targets before design.

SnapTarget answers the questions that come before any network is drawn: how much energy the process can recover, how much area that recovery needs, and which ΔTmin minimizes total annual cost. Everything is formula-driven — edit a stream and the targets, optima and charts update instantly.

  • Problem Table Algorithm energy & pinch targets
  • Bath-formula area and Euler units targets
  • ΔTmin trade-off study (0.5–40 °C) with two capital-cost models
  • Composite, shifted-composite & grand composite curves
  • Seven live charts, one per sheet — no macro re-runs
  • Self-resizing stream table with auto hot/cold detection
  • Validated to machine precision against the thesis Python model
