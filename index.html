About

Engineering, targeted.

I am a chemical engineer specializing in process integration and energy optimization. My graduate research delivered a complete heat exchanger network synthesis for a kerosene hydrotreater — from pinch-analysis targeting through rigorous MINLP superstructure with global optimization, cross-validated in Aspen Energy Analyzer.

SnapTera is where that work becomes practical tooling: "Snap" for speed, "Tera" for computational scale. Spreadsheet-native targeting software, optimization models, and consulting for plants that want to cut utility costs with confidence.

The Suite

Target. Optimize. Design. Advise.

One workflow, four modules: SnapTarget sets the energy targets, SnapOpt finds the cost-optimal network, SnapNet draws it — and SnapSage answers the engineering questions in between. SnapDox sits alongside them as a general-purpose file converter.
