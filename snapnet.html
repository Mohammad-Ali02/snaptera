What it does

From solution to drawing.

SnapNet reads the solution.json a SnapOpt run exports and draws the classic HEN grid diagram — hot streams on top, cold below, every exchanger annotated. What used to be an afternoon of careful hand-drawing becomes one command.

  • Automatic HEN grid diagram drawing from SnapOpt solutions
  • Duties, areas & stage temperatures annotated on the grid
  • Automatic split detection with duty fractions & mix temperatures
  • Utility heaters and coolers placed on the grid
  • Thesis-figure styling out of the box
  • Publication-ready PNG / SVG / PDF export
